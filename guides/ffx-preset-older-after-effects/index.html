Use a .ffx preset in an older After Effects

A preset saved in a newer After Effects won't apply in an older one, even if every effect in it exists in your version. The preset only stores which version saved it. Convert it to your version and apply the copy.

Convert one preset or a whole pack

  1. Open the converter and drop the .ffx files in. You can drop a whole folder.
  2. Pick your After Effects version (2021 to 2026).
  3. Download. One preset comes back as name.ae25.ffx (for 2025); several come back as one .zip. Presets that already work in your version are included unchanged.

Only two bytes in the file change. The presets stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

If the converted preset doesn't work

It probably uses an effect or property your version doesn't have. Changing the version stamp can't add it. Try the preset on a test layer first.
