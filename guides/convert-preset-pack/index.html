Guide

Convert a whole preset pack at once

Drop the whole folder into the converter, pick your After Effects version, and download everything as one .zip. You don't need to sort the files first.

What goes in the .zip

  • Converted: presets and projects saved in a newer version.
  • Included as is: files that already open in your version.
  • Kept as is: files Downframe can't convert, like presets from a version it doesn't recognise, or projects with a 2023 layer track matte going to 2022 or older.
  • Left out: files that aren't presets or projects. Each one is listed with the reason.

The .zip is named after your version, for example downframe-ae25.zip. A single file comes back on its own, for example name.ae25.ffx.

Before you convert

  • Add more files with "+ Add files", or remove one with ×.
  • You can mix .ffx presets and .aep projects.
  • Up to 500 MB per file and 1 GB per batch, since everything stays in your browser.
