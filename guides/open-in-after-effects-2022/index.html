Open a 2023–2026 file in After Effects 2022

After Effects 2022 (version 22.x) refuses projects and presets saved in 2023 or later. Convert the file to 2022 with Downframe and open the copy. Projects need more than a new version stamp here, and Downframe handles that for you.

Steps

  1. Open the converter and drop the .aep or .ffx in.
  2. Pick "After Effects 2022 (22.x)" and convert.
  3. Download the copy and open it in 2022.

Why projects are rebuilt

From 2023 on, After Effects stores every layer 4 bytes longer than 2022 does. Changing only the version stamp gives the error "chunk in file too big — probably unsupported file version (33 :: 5)". Downframe shortens every layer record back to the 2022 size and rebuilds the file around them. Presets don't need this.

What can't be converted

  • Layer track mattes. In 2023 any layer can be picked as a track matte. 2022 can't read that, so those projects are kept as they are. More on track mattes.
  • Newer features. Effects or properties that only exist in 2023 or later won't appear.

Converted files were tested in 2022 version 22.6. If one won't open in an earlier 2022 update, update to 22.6 or tell me.
