Open a 2026 file in After Effects 2025

After Effects 2025 (version 25.x) refuses projects and presets saved in 2026. Convert the file to 2025 with Downframe and open the copy. It works for .aep projects and .ffx presets, in your browser, for free.

Steps

  1. Open the converter and drop the file in. The list shows "Saved in After Effects 2026 (26.x)".
  2. Pick "After Effects 2025 (25.x)" and convert.
  3. Download the copy and open it in 2025.

Good to know

  • The copy opens in every 2025 update, not just the latest one.
  • From 2026 to 2025, only the version stamp changes. Anything that only exists in 2026 won't appear in 2025.
  • If the project uses third-party plugins, Downframe lists them first, because an older plugin can crash After Effects.
