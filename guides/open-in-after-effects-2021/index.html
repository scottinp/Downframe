Open a 2022–2026 file in After Effects 2021

After Effects 2021 is version 18.x, and it refuses projects and presets saved in any later version. Convert the file to 2021 with Downframe and open the copy. It works for .aep projects and .ffx presets, in your browser, for free.

Steps

  1. Open the converter and drop the file in. The list shows which version saved it.
  2. Pick "After Effects 2021 (18.x)" and convert.
  3. Download the copy and open it in 2021.

Good to know

  • Projects from 2023 or later are rebuilt. Those versions store every layer 4 bytes longer, so Downframe shortens each layer record to the 2021 size. Projects from 2022 only need the new version stamp.
  • Layer track mattes can't be converted. In 2023 any layer can be picked as a track matte. 2021 can't read that, so those projects are kept as they are. More on track mattes.
  • Newer features won't appear. Effects or properties that 2021 doesn't have can't be added.
