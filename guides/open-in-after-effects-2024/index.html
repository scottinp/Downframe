Open a 2025 or 2026 file in After Effects 2024

After Effects 2024 (version 24.x) refuses projects and presets saved in 2025 or 2026. Convert the file to 2024 with Downframe and open the copy. It works for .aep projects and .ffx presets, in your browser, for free.

Steps

  1. Open the converter and drop the file in. The list shows which version saved it.
  2. Pick "After Effects 2024 (24.x)" and convert.
  3. Download the copy and open it in 2024.

Good to know

  • The copy opens in every 2024 update, not just the latest one.
  • Going to 2024, only the version stamp changes. Anything that only exists in 2025 or 2026 won't appear.
  • If the project uses third-party plugins, Downframe lists them first, because an older plugin can crash After Effects.
