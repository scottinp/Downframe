Guide

Open an After Effects project in an older version

If you have the newer After Effects, use File → Save As → Save a Copy As, which saves for up to two versions back (2026 offers 25.x and 24.x). If you don't (a client or teammate sent the project), convert the .aep to your version with Downframe. It works from 2026 down to 2021.

Convert the project

  1. Open the converter and drop the .aep in. You can drop several at once and get one .zip.
  2. Check the list of third-party plugin effects, if any are shown. Disable them in the newer version first if you can.
  3. Pick your version, convert, and open the copy.

Projects up to 500 MB are fine. The file stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

What changes, and what doesn't

Downframe rewrites the version stamp. Projects saved in 2023 or later going to 2022 or 2021 are also rebuilt, because those versions store each layer 4 bytes shorter. Nothing else is touched, so effects your version doesn't have won't appear, and a layer that uses another layer as its track matte can't go to 2022 or older.
