Downloaded an After Effects template that won't open?

Most templates are saved in the newest After Effects, so an older version refuses them with "created with a newer version of After Effects". Convert the template's .aep to your version with Downframe and open the copy.

Steps

  1. Unzip the template download. The project is the .aep file inside, usually next to a footage or assets folder.
  2. Drop the .aep into the converter and pick your After Effects version.
  3. Save the converted copy in the same folder as the original, so the footage links still work, and open it.

If it still doesn't work

  • Required plugins. Templates often need plugins (the product page usually lists them). Downframe lists any third-party effects before converting. An older or missing plugin can crash After Effects. What to do.
  • Missing fonts or footage. These are separate from the version. After Effects tells you which fonts are missing, and you can relink footage with File → Dependencies.
  • Newer features. If the template relies on something your version doesn't have, it won't appear.
  • Not an .aep. Downframe converts .aep projects and .ffx presets only.
