Open a 2024, 2025 or 2026 file in After Effects 2023

After Effects 2023 (version 23.x) refuses projects and presets saved in any later version. Convert the file to 2023 with Downframe and open the copy. It works for .aep projects and .ffx presets, in your browser, for free.

Steps

  1. Open the converter and drop the file in. The list shows which version saved it.
  2. Pick "After Effects 2023 (23.x)" and convert.
  3. Download the copy and open it in 2023.

Good to know

  • The copy opens in every 2023 update, not just the latest one.
  • Going to 2023, only the version stamp changes. 2023 already stores layers the same way as later versions, so projects need no rebuilding.
  • Anything that only exists in 2024 or later won't appear in 2023.
  • If the project uses third-party plugins, Downframe lists them first, because an older plugin can crash After Effects.
